Meet Twin Cities HiFi

Hi, I’m Madhu — a practicing physician and long-time home theater enthusiast. I started Twin Cities HiFi in 2024 to help people in the Twin Cities build systems that feel at home in their spaces and sound even better than they expected.

Because this is a boutique, not a warehouse, I only carry brands and models I would happily use in my own living room or theater. That means well-matched components, sensible upgrades, and no gimmicks or hard sells.

Some of the brands I trust and recommend:

  • Denon & Marantz – Versatile AVRs and processors with excellent room correction options, including DIRAC support on select models.
  • Bowers & Wilkins (B&W) – High-performance speakers for both critical 2-channel listening and fully immersive home theater.
  • SVS – Powerful, controlled subwoofers that bring convincing, deep bass and impact to movies and music.
  • Kaleidescape – The gold-standard movie server platform for those who want the best possible audio and video quality at home.
  • Furman & Panamax – Power conditioning and surge protection to safeguard your investment over the long haul.
  • RTI Control – Flexible control systems that make complex setups feel simple and intuitive. RTI is locally owned and headquartered in Shakopee, MN.

All products are sold as an authorized dealer, with full manufacturer warranties and local support from someone who genuinely cares about your system.

Common Questions

If you’re just starting to plan your system — or upgrading from a soundbar — these are a few things people often ask before reaching out.

Do you charge for consultations?

No. A quick call, email, or text to discuss your room, budget, and goals is always free. If you’re local, we can also talk about whether a demo would be helpful.

Is there a physical showroom?

Yes. I maintain a private demo space in Roseville, MN. It’s not a walk-in store — all demos are by appointment so we can focus on your system without distractions.

Can you help me choose gear for my exact room?

Absolutely. The brands listed here cover a wide range of rooms and use-cases. Share your room dimensions, seating, and preferences, and I’ll recommend a few well-matched options.

Can you work with some of my existing gear?

In many cases, yes. If you already have components you like, we can evaluate what to keep, what to upgrade, and how to get the most from what you own.
