Meet Twin Cities HiFi

Hi, I'm Madhu — a practicing physician and long-time home theater enthusiast. I started Twin Cities HiFi in 2024 to help people in the Twin Cities build systems that look great in their homes and sound even better.

Because this is a passion project and not a high-pressure retail showroom, I only carry brands and models I would happily use in my own living room or theater. That means well-matched components, sensible upgrades, and no gimmicks.

Some of the brands I trust and recommend:

  • Denon and Marantz offer a wide range of AVRs and processors to fit many budgets and listening goals. They also support DIRAC room correction, which rivals the best room correction suites available.
  • Bowers & Wilkins (B&W) delivers stunning performance for both critical 2-channel listening and fully immersive home theater setups.
  • SVS is known for legendary subwoofers that add deep, controlled bass and that satisfying chest-thump to movie soundtracks.
  • Kaleidescape is the ultimate video source for discerning cinephiles who want the best possible audio and video quality at home.
  • Furman and Panamax provide robust power conditioning and surge protection to help protect your investment for the long haul.
  • RTI control systems tie everything together so a complex system feels simple and intuitive for everyone in the family. They’re also locally owned with HQ in Shakopee.

I’m authorized for everything I sell, so you can buy with confidence knowing you’re fully covered by the manufacturer’s warranty and supported by someone who genuinely cares about your system.

Common Questions

If you’re just starting to plan your system — or upgrading from a soundbar — these are a few things people often ask before reaching out.

Do you charge for consultations?

No. A quick call, email, or text to discuss your room, budget, and goals is always free. If you’re local, we can also talk about how a demo might help narrow things down.

Is there a physical showroom?

Yes — I have a private demo space in Roseville, MN. It’s not a walk-in store; all demos are by appointment so we can focus on your system without distractions.

Can you help me choose gear for my exact room?

Absolutely. The brands listed here cover a wide range of rooms and use-cases. Share your room dimensions, seating, and preferences, and I’ll recommend a few well-matched paths forward.
