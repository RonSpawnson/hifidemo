Carefully selected for performance, reliability, and support — not to fill shelves. All products include full manufacturer warranties and local support from someone who actually listens to your system with you.

Brands I trust and why
  • Denon & Marantz offer a wide range of AVRs and processors to fit many budgets and listening goals. With options for DIRAC room correction, they rival the best room correction suites available today.
  • Bowers & Wilkins delivers stunning performance for both critical 2-channel listening and fully immersive home theater setups.
  • SVS is known for legendary subwoofers that add deep, controlled bass and that satisfying “chest thump” to movies.
  • Kaleidescape is the ultimate video source for cinephiles who want reference-level audio and video quality at home.
  • Furman & Panamax provide robust power conditioning and surge protection to help protect your investment over the long term.
  • RTI control systems tie everything together so a complex system feels simple and intuitive for everyone in the family. They’re also locally owned with headquarters in Shakopee.

Common questions

A few quick answers. If you’re unsure about anything, just ask — I’d rather over-explain than leave you guessing.

Are you an authorized dealer?

Yes. I’m authorized for everything I sell, so you get the full manufacturer’s warranty plus a local partner who actually cares how your system sounds.

Do you work by appointment only?

Yes. Private, appointment-only demos in Roseville let us focus on your system without the noise and distractions of a big-box showroom.

Can you help if I already own some equipment?

Absolutely. Many people come in with an existing AVR or speakers. I’m happy to help with system matching, sensible upgrades, and setup.

Do you only do very high-end systems?

No. I focus on mid to upper-end systems, but we can start where it makes sense for you — often with a path to grow over time instead of one huge purchase.
