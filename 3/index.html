About Twin Cities HiFi

A small, owner-operated shop focused on systems that feel at home in your space and deliver a genuine step up in performance.

Because this is a passion project and not a big-box showroom, I only carry brands and combinations I would happily use in my own systems. That means honest guidance, sensible upgrades, and no pressure.

Some of the brands I trust and why:

  • Denon and Marantz offer a wide range of AVRs and processors to fit many budgets and listening goals. With options for DIRAC room correction, they rival the best room correction suites available today.
  • Bowers & Wilkins delivers stunning performance for both critical 2-channel listening and fully immersive home theater setups.
  • SVS is known for legendary subwoofers that add deep, controlled bass and that satisfying “chest thump” to movies.
  • Kaleidescape is the ultimate video source for cinephiles who want reference-level audio and video quality at home.
  • Furman and Panamax provide robust power conditioning and surge protection to help protect your investment over the long term.
  • RTI control systems tie everything together so a complex system feels simple and intuitive for everyone in the family. They’re also locally owned with headquarters in Shakopee.

I’m authorized for everything I sell, so you get the full manufacturer’s warranty plus a local partner who actually cares how your system sounds.
