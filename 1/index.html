Common questions

A few quick answers. If you’re unsure about anything, just ask—we’re happy to walk through details.

Are you an authorized dealer?

Yes. We are an authorized dealer for all the brands listed above, and all new products carry the full manufacturer warranty.

Do you work by appointment only?

Yes. Demos and in-person meetings are by appointment so we can give you unhurried time and keep the space focused on listening.

Can you help if I already own some equipment?

Absolutely. Many clients come to us with an existing AVR or speakers. We can help with system matching, upgrades, and setup.

Do you only do expensive systems?

No. We’re happy to start where it makes sense for you, often with an eye toward smart upgrades over time instead of one huge purchase.
