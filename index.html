Twin Cities HiFi - About Us

Hi, I'm Madhu the owner of Twin Cities HiFi. I'm a physician by profession, and a long time home theater enthusiast. I started Twin Cities HiFi in 2024 with an aim to sell curated products that fit well in mid to upper end living room systems.

  • Denon and Marantz offer a wide range of AVR's and Processors to fit any budget and listening goal. They have the option to offer DIRAC room correction, which rivals the best room correction suites
  • Bowers and Wilkins offers stunning performance, whether for critical 2ch listening or for immersive home theater
  • SVS is known for their legendary subwoofers. Adding that deep bass and chest thump to your home theater
  • Kaleidescape is the ultimate video source for the discerning cinephile
  • Furman and Panamax offer a wide range of solutions to protect your investment from power surges and spikes
  • RTI control systems ties everything together to make a complicated system easily operated by anyone in the family. Plus, they are locally owned with their HQ in Shakopee

I'm authorized for everything I sell, so you have the peace of mind knowing you are covered by the full manufacturer's warranty
